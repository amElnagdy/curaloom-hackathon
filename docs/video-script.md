Shot list v2, Nov 4. It replaces draft 1, which followed the advanced path and had no diagram. The video is at most 3:00 with an English voiceover. The on-screen Arabic is quoted as the app shows it on the intermediate path of `pnpm demo:replay`. Each string below was captured from that path on Nov 4, after #126.

## Before recording

1. Restart `pnpm demo:replay` from main after #128. An older build moves the window 56 px when a quiz result or the re-plan proposal arrives, which hides the top bar. A build from before #126 also scrolls the page away on the diagram toggle.
2. Reset before every take. Wait for «لا يعمل أي وكيل الآن», then click «إعادة» and «نعم، ابدأ من الصفر». The clock must read «يوم البدء ٩:٠٠ ص».
3. Open lesson 2 from «تابع إلى الدرس الثاني» right after the quiz pass. That order replays with no missing recordings, which was checked at all three levels.
4. Give consent before the clock moves. Otherwise the day 2 and day 5 rows show «لم تُرسل قبل موافقتك».
5. Record at a 1600×900 viewport, which is 16:9, at 2× device scale. Keep browser zoom at 100%, since a shorter window collapses the demo bar.
6. Use one tab, and hide the translate prompt, the bookmarks bar and the extensions. Keep the terminal, file paths and `.env` out of frame.
7. Record each row group as its own clip: 1–2, 3, 4–5, 6, 8–9 and 10. Reset only between full takes.

## Risks

1. **The quiz's first attempt must fail.** Replay finds each grade by its request, so the first attempt has to use the e2e's picks and grade ٦٧٪. Other answers have no recording.
2. **The diagram image loads lazily.** Wait for it to draw before toggling, or the frame shows grey.
3. **Quiz results fade in from a blur.** Start the hold about 1 s after the result appears.
4. **Two week figures disagree on screen.** The proposal strip shows «الأسبوع الأول ١٦٠ ← ٥٠ دقيقة» for days 0 to 6, while the stats and the re-planner's note give ٦٠ دقيقة for the light week of days 10–16. Both are right for their windows, but a frame with both invites the question. Keep the strip out of the zoom.
5. **The official rules.** The 3:00 limit and the 30/25/20/25 weights come from #31 and secondary sources. Check the hackathon's Official Rules before the upload.
